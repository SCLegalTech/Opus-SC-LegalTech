# Commercial Use

Opus is fair-code/source-available. The self-hosted edition is intended for
internal use, evaluation, education, research, development, and law-firm
operations controlled by the self-hoster.

You need a separate commercial license from Smart Crypto Legal S.A.S. if you
want to:

- offer Opus as hosted or managed software to third parties;
- resell access to Opus, its MCP tools, workflows, UI, or APIs;
- white-label or rebrand Opus;
- embed Opus as a substantial part of another SaaS, marketplace, legal
  operations product, workflow automation product, or MCP platform;
- operate legal files, workflows, credentials, or client data for third parties
  on infrastructure controlled by you;
- use Opus as a substantial component of a competing commercial offering.
