# Code of Conduct

We want the Opus community to be professional, respectful, and useful.

Expected behavior:

- be respectful and constructive;
- assume good intent while staying precise;
- keep discussions focused on the software and its documentation;
- avoid sharing confidential client, court, case, credential, or personal data;
- respect the fair-code license and closed-module boundaries.

Unacceptable behavior:

- harassment, threats, insults or discrimination;
- publishing secrets, personal data, court credentials or client information;
- pressuring maintainers for free private support;
- using issues or PRs to request help bypassing legal, ethical or platform rules;
- submitting code that automates third-party systems without authorization.

Maintainers may moderate, edit, close, delete, or block participation when needed
to protect the project and its users.
